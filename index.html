The problem

A lethal finding hiding in scans already taken

Aortic aneurysms kill because they go undetected. Not because the evidence isn't there — it is, in the millions of routine abdominal CTs performed every year.

🫀

Silent until it's catastrophic

An abdominal aortic aneurysm causes no pain, no symptoms, and no warning signs. The first indication is often rupture — by which point 80% of patients do not survive to reach the operating table.

🔍

Already visible, never measured

Studies show that patients who later die from rupture had the aorta clearly visible in scans taken years earlier — for kidney stones, cancer follow-ups, abdominal pain. The scan was there. The measurement was not.

📏

The diagnosis is just a measurement

Unlike most diseases, AAA doesn't require a learned model or a complex classifier. The aortic diameter in millimetres — compared against a threshold — is the diagnosis. It is pure geometry.

🚫

Existing AI tools don't cover this

Every FDA-cleared aortic AI tool operates on dedicated contrast-enhanced aortic CTA — scans ordered because a clinician already suspects the problem. The opportunistic angle, on routine non-vascular CT, is completely unaddressed.

The pipeline

Segment. Measure. Flag.

Marrowen's architecture is the simplest possible: no trained classifier, no probabilistic model. The biomarker is geometric and the threshold is fixed. Accuracy comes from the measurement, not the algorithm.

1

Ingest & Filter

DICOM metadata is read. Series flagged as contrast-enhanced or non-abdominal are excluded. The remaining scan is converted and resampled for analysis.

2

Aorta Segmentation

TotalSegmentator extracts the full aortic volume with published Dice >0.97. An ellipse is fitted to each axial slice. Maximum outer-wall diameter is recorded with the vertebral level of the widest point.

3

Flag & Report

Diameter is compared against clinical thresholds. A one-page PDF is generated per patient: max diameter, location, threshold comparison, and recommended action — ready to append to the existing report.

Output

One measurement, one clear action

Every scan produces a maximum diameter, an anatomical location, and an unambiguous clinical recommendation. No score, no probability — just the number and what it means.

Finding Max diameter Rupture risk Recommended action
Normal Below 30 mm Negligible No action — note in report
Small AAA 30 – 44 mm Low (<1% / year) Ultrasound surveillance every 2–3 years
Medium AAA 45 – 54 mm Moderate (~1–3% / year) Vascular surgery referral — surveillance every 6–12 months
Large AAA 55 mm or above High (>5% / year) Urgent vascular referral — surgical repair discussion
